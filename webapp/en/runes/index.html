ᚠ Runes Elder Futhark

24 runes · meanings, divination, magic, and diagnostics

Runes are more than archaic epigraphic signs or elements of a pre-Christian alphabet. In a synthesis of academic discourse and modern divination, runes are viewed as ‘universal energies’ and a kind of ‘programming language of the Higher Realms.’ According to the concept of mentally programming the Universe, the runic system is a high-frequency instrument capable of changing any lower-order system, from physiological processes in the body to global chains of events. For a modern person, mastering this ‘language’ is a path toward harmony with the Genius of the Earth and the collective unconscious. Every practitioner should be able to conduct the currents of all 24 Elder Futhark runes through themselves. An inability to interact with these archetypes, or failure to work through them, leads to discord in life. A rune magician is like a musician: if they ignore the laws of the score of the universe, life becomes a cacophony instead of an act of creation. The importance of precision in interpretation and inscription was already emphasized in Egil’s Saga: ‘None should carve runes except those who interpret them well. Unclear staves have confused many people.’ This reference is intended to separate the grain of archaeological fact, drawing on the work of E. A. Melnikova, from the chaff of amateur speculation and to provide an in-depth analysis of each symbol.
