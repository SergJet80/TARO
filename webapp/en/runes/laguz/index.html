ᛚ Laguz Laguz

Third aett · sound ‘l’ · water, lake

The rune of flow, sensitivity, and movement through a changing environment. It is associated with intuition, emotion, and the ability to feel a situation’s current rather than break it through direct pressure.

Core meaning

The rune of flow, sensitivity, and movement through a changing environment. It is associated with intuition, emotion, and the ability to feel a situation’s current rather than break it through direct pressure.

Historical tradition

The name is associated with water or a lake; the rune poems develop the image of water and the sea. Modern divination applies this image to emotional flow, intuition, subconscious reactions, and flexibility.

  • Key meanings: water, flow, intuition, feelings, flexibility, depth, perception, current

Modern divination

These divinatory meanings belong to modern runic practice.

  • Positive expression: Good intuition, natural development, emotional depth, or the ability to adapt without losing the goal.
  • Negative expression: Illusions, emotional instability, self-deception, or passively drifting wherever the current carries you.
  • Advice: Listen to your feelings, but test them against reality; flexibility should not replace choice.
  • Warning: A strong feeling is not yet evidence.
  • Outcome: The situation will develop through adaptation to changing conditions rather than according to a rigid plan.

Psychological state

High receptivity; the task is to preserve the boundary between an inner feeling and an external fact.

Spiritual meaning

Trusting the current of life without relinquishing responsibility for one’s own choice.

Upright and reversed positions

Divinatory interpretation and the use of reversed positions belong to modern runic practice. No ancient manual survives for the Elder Futhark that establishes this method of divination as the only correct one. In modern schools, reversed Laguz means emotional confusion, faulty intuition, self-deception, manipulation of feelings, or movement against the natural course.

Differences between schools

Water as the original image is historical; ‘women’s magic,’ love-spell functions, and work with the subconscious are modern esoteric developments.

Summary

The rune of flow, sensitivity, and movement through a changing environment. It is associated with intuition, emotion, and the ability to feel a situation’s current rather than break it through direct pressure.

Finances

A variable cash flow or income through flexibility and an intuitive sense of the market; do not make financial decisions solely on emotion.

Work

A creative or flexible environment, work with people or information flows, or changing conditions.

Relationships

Deep feelings, emotional involvement, and a strong intuitive connection; in the shadow, idealization.

Family and home

The emotional climate at home, flexible adaptation to change, and themes of water and travel.

Rune as a description of a person

A sensitive, intuitive, flexible person who notices nuance and atmosphere.

  • Thoughts: They rely more on impressions and subtext than on a rigid scheme.
  • Feelings: Deep, fluid, and sometimes contradictory.
  • Actions: They will adapt, observe, and choose the right moment instead of applying direct pressure.

Modern magical meaning

This is modern esoteric practice, not a historically attested tradition.

  • Modern magical interpretation: Intuition, emotional influence, flexibility, symbolic cleansing through water, and movement of a flow.
  • Purposes: Strengthening sensitivity, work with relationships, finding a gentle path, or adaptation.
  • Strengthens: Fluidity, receptivity, and emotional involvement.
  • Weakens: Rigidity and stillness.
  • Risk: Blurring the boundaries of the goal or strengthening emotional dependence.

Rune in formulas and staves

Laguz often sets a gentle flow: Laguz+Gebo — emotional reciprocity; Laguz+Ansuz — intuitive communication; Laguz+Raidho — movement through changing conditions.

Esoteric interpretation

In modern esoteric interpretation

In modern diagnostic systems, a strained Laguz is sometimes associated with emotional suggestion, ‘bewilderment,’ or influence on feelings.

Possible everyday explanation

Alternative explanation

An everyday alternative: idealization, emotional manipulation by an actual person, anxiety, things left unsaid, or misinterpretation of one’s own feelings.

Characteristic combinations

  • Laguz + Gebo: An emotional connection and reciprocity.
  • Laguz + Perthro: Mystery, intuition, and uncertainty.
  • Laguz + Isa: A halt in the emotional flow.
