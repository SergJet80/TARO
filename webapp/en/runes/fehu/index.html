ᚠ Fehu Fehu

First aett · sound ‘f’ · cattle, movable wealth

The rune of resources, possession, and the circulation of material goods. In modern divination it shows what a person has at their disposal and how well they can retain, increase, and set a resource in motion.

Core meaning

The rune of resources, possession, and the circulation of material goods. In modern divination it shows what a person has at their disposal and how well they can retain, increase, and set a resource in motion.

Historical tradition

The name *fehu is associated with cattle as a measure of movable wealth. The Anglo-Saxon Rune Poem directly links Feoh with wealth and the need to share it in order to earn social recognition. Early runic inscriptions often record names, affiliation, and ownership; the broader interpretation of Fehu as a ‘flow of money’ belongs to modern esoteric schools.

  • Key meanings: resource, property, prosperity, value, exchange, possession, movement, result

Modern divination

These divinatory meanings belong to modern runic practice.

  • Positive expression: Receiving a resource, a successful acquisition, growth of material support, and the ability to manage what is available.
  • Negative expression: Greed, dependence on material things, loss of control over spending, or a conflict over property.
  • Advice: Assess your actual resources and put them to work; do not hold on to property solely out of fear of loss.
  • Warning: Possession is not the same as security: a resource requires management and exchange.
  • Outcome: A material result, profit, an acquisition, or the securing of something valuable.

Psychological state

A sense of being resourced and able to act; in the shadow, fear of poverty and a desire to control everything through ownership.

Spiritual meaning

The lesson of responsibility for what one owns: a resource becomes power only through movement and meaningful exchange.

Upright and reversed positions

Divinatory interpretation and the use of reversed positions belong to modern runic practice. No ancient manual survives for the Elder Futhark that establishes this method of divination as the only correct one. In modern schools, reversed Fehu is often read as loss, poor management of resources, delayed payments, or devaluation. There is no historical rule for reversed runes.

Differences between schools

The historical layer is cattle or wealth and social value; modern divination expands this to money, energy, and resources in general. The magical function of an ‘accumulator’ is a later practical model.

Summary

The rune of resources, possession, and the circulation of material goods. In modern divination it shows what a person has at their disposal and how well they can retain, increase, and set a resource in motion.

Finances

Money coming in, profit, the return of a resource, or a useful acquisition. In its shadow, wastefulness or loss.

Work

A paid result, an increase in a specialist’s value, a successful commercial project, or adaptation to the environment.

Relationships

A wish to create a stable couple and shared home life; also the theme of mutual value and each person’s contribution.

Family and home

Property, purchases, housekeeping, and strengthening the material foundation of the home.

Rune as a description of a person

A practical, resourceful person focused on a concrete result and a sense of personal space.

  • Thoughts: They assess the benefit, the prospects, and what they can obtain or preserve.
  • Feelings: They value stability and tangible reciprocity; at times they express feelings through care and material contribution.
  • Actions: They will strengthen their position, invest, buy, negotiate, or protect what is theirs.

Modern magical meaning

This is modern esoteric practice, not a historically attested tradition.

  • Modern magical interpretation: Attracting and securing resources and strengthening the material side of a matter.
  • Purposes: Money, sales, property, the launch of a material project, or supplying a formula with energy.
  • Strengthens: Inflow, growth, and the ability to retain what has been received.
  • Weakens: Stagnation, passivity, and the dissipation of resources.
  • Risk: Excessive fixation on material things or an attempt to accelerate a process without a system.

Rune in formulas and staves

In modern formulas, Fehu is often placed as a ‘resource engine’ or as the sign of a desired material result. Illustrative examples: Fehu+Gebo — mutually beneficial exchange; Fehu+Raidho — movement of money or a trade route; Fehu+Othala — property and a family asset.

Esoteric interpretation

In modern esoteric interpretation

In modern diagnostic schools, an obstructed Fehu may be read as a ‘money block,’ a leak of resources, or someone else’s influence on the material sphere.

Possible everyday explanation

Alternative explanation

An everyday alternative: budgeting errors, unstable income, fear of social contact, or an unsuccessful business model. Fehu alone does not prove any external influence.

Characteristic combinations

  • Fehu + Gebo: Exchange, a gift, or a beneficial partnership.
  • Fehu + Nauthiz: Shortage, forced expenses, or a resource under pressure.
  • Fehu + Othala: Property, inheritance, or investment in a home.
