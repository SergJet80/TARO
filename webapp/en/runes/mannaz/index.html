Core meaning

The rune of a human being as an individual and a member of society. It shows self-esteem, one’s role in a group, the influence of the social environment, and th

Historical tradition

The name means a human being. The rune poems develop the themes of human life and belonging to a community. Modern divination expands Mannaz to self-knowledge, social roles, intelligence, and collective support.

  • Key meanings: human, society, individual, reason, help, role, self-knowledge, collective

Modern divination

These divinatory meanings belong to modern runic practice.

  • Positive expression: Support from people, useful cooperation, a clear understanding of one’s role, or a rational decision.
  • Negative expression: Egocentrism, conflict with a group, dependence on others’ opinions, loneliness, or social isolation.
  • Advice: Look at the situation through the eyes of the other participants and assess your own role without self-deception.
  • Warning: Do not treat your own point of view as the only human and rational one.
  • Outcome: The result depends on people, reputation, the team, and the ability to fit properly into a social system.

Psychological state

Self-reflection and social identity; in the shadow, excessive dependence on other people’s judgment.

Spiritual meaning

Self-knowledge through interaction: a person sees their boundaries and qualities especially clearly beside others.

Upright and reversed positions

Divinatory interpretation and the use of reversed positions belong to modern runic practice. No ancient manual survives for the Elder Futhark that establishes this method of divination as the only correct one. In modern practice, reversed Mannaz means misunderstanding oneself, conflict with society, inaccurate self-esteem, or an unpleasant human factor.

Differences between schools

The historical theme ‘human being’ is clear; modern meanings involving intellect, psychology, and mass influence are expansions, not an ancient dictionary.

Rune as a description of a person

A rational, socially oriented person who understands themselves through relationships with others.

  • Thoughts: They analyze themselves and others’ reactions and compare positions.
  • Feelings: Respect, recognition, and the sense of being understood as an individual are important.
  • Actions: They will approach people, ask for help, cooperate, or reconsider their own role.

Modern magical meaning

This is modern esoteric practice, not a historically attested tradition.

  • Modern magical interpretation: Work with the individual, social interaction, learning, and collective influence.
  • Purposes: Communication with a group, finding helpers, strengthening one’s personal position, or working with reputation.
  • Strengthens: Social ties, awareness of one’s role, and rational interaction.
  • Weakens: Isolation and the rupture between a person and the group.
  • Risk: Moving from improving communication to trying to control other people’s opinions.

Rune in formulas and staves

Mannaz often represents the person or a group: Mannaz+Ansuz — communication or learning; Mannaz+Gebo — cooperation; Mannaz+Algiz — protection of a person.

Esoteric interpretation

In modern esoteric interpretation

In diagnostics, Mannaz may represent the querent, a particular person, or the social environment; strained combinations are sometimes interpreted as external human influence.

Possible everyday explanation

Alternative explanation

An everyday alternative: an actual conflict with a person, group pressure, manipulative communication, or dependence on others’ opinions.

Characteristic combinations

  • Mannaz + Gebo: A partnership between people.
  • Mannaz + Ansuz: Communication, learning, or advice.
  • Mannaz + Nauthiz: A person under severe social or everyday pressure.
