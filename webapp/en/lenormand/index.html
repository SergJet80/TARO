🂠 Lenormand Oracle · 36 cards

meanings by area of life · timing · 1,260 combinations

Lenormand decks have roots in the eighteenth century and owe their name and enduring appeal to the celebrated French fortune-teller Marie Lenormand, who read cards for prominent figures of her time. The familiar Petit Lenormand has 36 cards with recognisable everyday symbols: the Fox, Ship, Sun, Tree and others. Their direct imagery makes an approachable starting point for learning card reading. Compared with more elaborate systems such as Tarot, Lenormand readings tend to focus on concrete situations and practical questions. Readers may seek quick, clear answers, but the cards do not establish facts or reliably predict events. The historical relationship between the deck and its namesake is explained in the interactive guide.

Card polarity is a working guide, not a verdict: neighbouring cards and the question shape the tone of a reading.
