🂠 Playing-card inset and details

The Snake — Queen of Clubs. Polarity: negative. Timing: with delays and detours

Complicates a direct route and introduces a hidden motive. A more nuanced reading can also suggest flexibility, strategy, physical desire and the ability to act indirectly.

🏠 Everyday life

detour, confusing route, cable, medicine, jealousy, complicated arrangement.

💼 Work and business

office politics, complex deal, competitor, supply chain, cyber threat, negotiating tactics.

❤ Relationships

temptation, jealousy, love triangle, strong sexuality, manipulation or a complicated female partner.

👤 Personality

intelligent, adaptable, sensual, strategic, secretive, jealous, sometimes vindictive.

📍 Objects and places

snake, cable, hose, hairpin bends, pharmacy, winding road.

🎲 Unusual and literal expressions

medicine and pharmaceuticals; yoga; toxic environment; complex code; a chain of intermediaries.

☾ Shadow side

betrayal, being ruled by desire, intrigue, toxic competition, deliberate distortion.

★ Practical advice

Avoid forcing your way through. Check motives, intermediaries and the true cost of an attractive offer.

⏳ Timing note

with delays and detours. Timing conventions differ between Lenormand schools; choose one system before applying them.
