XIPage of Swords

Suit: Swords  ·  Element: Air

curiositynewsletterteachingalertnesssharp-headedobservation

The young man with his sword raised looks back at the wind: a curiosity and vigilance greedy for knowledge, not yet knowing discipline.

✧ Archetype and core meaning

The page stands on a hill with a sword in the test stand; the wind rattles the hair, the look goes back to follow what is happening around. This is the spirit of the Air at a young age: the mind is greedy, fast, almost unassembled. The student and the scout, he collects information, tries on ideas, asks questions that adults have long ceased to ask.

Its strength is the freshness of its eyes: it notices what others have been exposed to, and it hears the news that will soon become common; weakness is absent-mindedness: interests change faster than any course started ends; as a signal of alignment, the card brings news that require attention but not haste, and favors everything that begins with the word “interesting”.

🏠 Daily life

The unexpected news, the fascinating topic, the sticky learning of something new, from recipe to language, the calls, the correspondence, the paperwork, the short trips are good. Write down ideas: there are many, but they are volatile. Check the rumors from the source - the information from third-hand comes distorted.

💼 Work and career

Professional start or upgrade: new position, internship, first client, mastering the tool. Beginner's position now has the advantage of asking questions, collecting insiders, making contacts. There may be news of a vacancy or a competition. Advice: pick one topic for deep entry - widespread curiosity without focus dissolves the result.

❤ Relationships

The romance of the mind: flirting in correspondence, talking until the morning, meeting where it is interesting to talk first. The relationship in the intelligence phase is both watching and teasing. The pair comes up with a fresh common theme: joint course, travel-research. Careful: spying on your partner is a bad sport, trust is more important than awareness.

🌿 Health

Good diagnostic time: examination, consultation, second opinion — information about your own body is obtained easily and on time. Keep a journal of well-being: observation reveals patterns that you can not see in fifteen minutes of reception. There is a lot of energy, but it is impulsive: warm-up is required, otherwise enthusiasm will end in stretching.

🔮 Spiritual meaning

Apprenticeship: the learning period, the search for a school or teacher, the first self-practice, patronizing beginner fortune tellers and anyone who keeps observational diaries; synchronicity is taught to read the world as text; the protective side is information hygiene: filter what you let into the field; the word of the beginner already has weight.

The wind carries words: chatter instead of action, surveillance instead of interest, and a sharp tongue that cuts before the head thinks.

✧ Archetype and core meaning

The same wind, but without a rudder. Word became a weapon without a sight: gossip, messages sent before the thoughtful, sharp joke wrong there. Much is said, little is listened to, everything is known and nothing is profound.

Watching has become surveillance: controlling other people's pages, reading correspondence, questioning under the guise of care -- curiosity is spying on the lives of others instead of building its own. The third story is spraying: ten courses started, none continued; knowledge is collected with stickers and does not become a skill. The card is not cruel: immaturity is a stage, not a sentence. But movement without development has a price.

🏠 Daily life

The gossip of relatives, the chain of messages that eats the evening, the purchase influenced by random review. Words diverge quickly and expensively — said is a long time to rake in, pause before sending any sharp message, filter for news. It is more useful to silently finish one thing than to tell about five future.

💼 Work and career

Office chatter is backfired: gossip comes back to the source, confidential information comes up where it shouldn't. The reputation of a person who doesn't keep his mouth shut rises. The other risk is imitation: courses without application, research without conclusions. One closed item blocks ten announcements.

❤ Relationships

The slutty in the guise of wit: the partner is tired of being a stand-up audience; the jealous investigation — checking the phone, questioning the past — replaces trust with control; the intimacy doesn't grow; the single person has correspondence that doesn't turn into meetings: virtual flirtation is safer and therefore endless; the recipe for growing up is to talk about fears directly, not bite and peek.

🌿 Health

Information overeating is a nerve-wracking thing: mood swings, trouble falling asleep because of tape, nervous coughing. Voice and throat are the weak link: breakdowns from talking. Traumatic rush: cuts and bruises due to inattention, sports without warm-ups. Digital posts — hours without a phone in the morning and before bedtime — help.

🔮 Spiritual meaning

Magical immaturity: the practitioner is thrown in the middle, the techniques are mixed without a system, words are spoken lightly, and they have consequences. The danger of talking about other people's practices and secrets without asking. The treatment is strict: one tradition, one practice on a schedule, a vow of silence about what is being done. The strength of the student's word grows exactly in proportion to the ability to hold it.

The main idea

He's already holding a sword, but his job is simple: to understand the order correctly and to carry it out.

How it manifests

In work, you're new to a strict hierarchy, in learning, you have a basic level of knowledge, in communication, you literally have to hear what you're asked to do.

The shadow side

The order is misunderstood and the actions become hectic and ineffective.

📜 In detail — from the rank lecture

✧ Essence of the card

The dependence on information and power: "a man with a sword gives orders, you can not disobey a man with a sword"; "knowledge is power." The page already holds information, but not deep.

☀ Upright position
  • work: the entire hospital hierarchy - the chief physician king, the heads of the queens, ordinary doctors and surgeons knights, nurses and page staff; page swords - "more patient than doctor"; also scientists and teachers who transmit knowledge and "forceful method" require to teach.
  • Obligation: the district calls about a drunken son - "there is no question of any desire", only the obligation to flee to help out, from which "it is difficult to get out as a winner."
  • Cognition: to steer in a new city, roughly understanding the route, straight; to drive "to the touch" - upside down. You know the language "four, closer to three": you can understand, you can't ask - ask for initiative.
☾ Negative meaning

The page's main task is to "understand the order correctly: how it understands it, and does it." In communication, Gemini is like: any common topic will support in a bad way; the inverted will keep silent on an unfamiliar topic.

★ School emphases and distinctive points
  • One scene is laid out in all four suits: the girl whistles after - the habit of not turning around (pentacles), the impulse to snap (wands), the calculation of "trolling" and ignoring a strong person (swords), emotional anticipation (cups); "you turn around - means page".
  • He illustrates the hierarchy of the court with one plot: the son does not get on the plane because of the weather - the page sits at the airport and waits, the knight changes the ticket, the queen chooses the transport herself, the king would fly in a private jet.
  • The teacher is smeared in the elements: information is swords, the skill is “driven into habit” – earth, socialization (“school is primarily a treasure trove of communication”) – wands, love and humanities – cups; class favorites – queens.
  • The medieval gender framework is openly stated: tarot is a “slice of medieval society”, women are traditionally put in the place of the page – the first to not show initiative.
  • Customer Reception: always add “but it’s inaccurate – cards work differently for different people, check on yourself first.”
  • Modern images: the film "Terminal" - a clean page (stuck at the airport, limited in everything); about Gagarin - "the party said it was necessary, the Komsomol answered to eat" - like an inverted page of pentacles, physically fixed.
  • Astrology (Zodiac, planet) and Slavic series at the lecture is not at all; outside the masty bindings only temperaments and comparison with Gemini.
  • Pregnancy is played by pages as an extreme dependence on the situation; alcohol and drug addiction are “also pages”, each has a dependence in his element.
  • If page drops when contracting, it is pointless to advise “learning”: the page has no choice – to be the perfect performer.
  • Take only visually pleasing deck; the course deck is intentionally neutral, and the Manara with naked bodies will cause rejection and will not work.
