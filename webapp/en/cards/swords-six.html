VISix of Swords

Suit: Swords  ·  Element: Air  ·  Traditional attribution: Mercury in Aquarius

transitionmovestorm-walkcalmingpastcrossing

The boat moves from the stormy waters to the quiet shore: a crossing through the inevitable, where sadness about what is left is mixed with quiet relief.

☉ Golden Dawn tradition

«The Lord of Earned Success» — Lord of Deserved Success

Mercury gets a systematic and detached intelligence in Aquarius, and the six harmonizes suit: the output is not through emotion, but through intelligent method, calculation and route.

✧ Archetype and core meaning

The carrier rules the boat silently, the passengers sit with their backs in their capes, and six swords stand in the boat - the luggage of what they are taking away. Behind the stern, the water is smooth ahead. It's a transition without heroism: a quiet, almost mournful escape from a situation that has become impossible.

Retreat is wiser than winning: moving out of a city where everything reminds you of loss, a new job after burnout, a new shore of the golden mountains is not expected — there is simply calmer, and often it is exactly what you need. Swords in the boat remind you that experience can not be left on the other shore. The crossing turns trauma into a biography if you let it swim.

🏠 Daily life

After a noisy streak, life gets straight: fixed, moved, disassembled boxes. Good little change of scenery -- reshuffle, trip to the water, new route. Literally move possible. A slight melancholy is normal: you pass an entire era of life; treated with hot tea and a to-do list in a new place.

💼 Work and career

Change of scenery: transfer to another department, smooth exit from the exhausted project, the place where it is more calm, even a step below. Conflicts subside themselves, you should leave the war zone. Move with respect for what you have passed: recommendations and connections will soon come in handy.

❤ Relationships

Relationships go from storm to calm — quietly, with a glance. A couple can move, start with a clean slate or learn to speak quietly. The opposite story can also be possible: peaceful spending without scenes — a boat takes everyone to their shore. Single people — acquaintance on the road or with a person from afar.

🌿 Health

The recovery is smooth: the acute phase is behind, the body is moving to stability. It's good to change the climate or the doctor - the treatment works better in the new place. It shows water: pool, sea trips, baths. Don't demand instant happiness: the crossing does not tolerate rush.

🔮 Spiritual meaning

Guide card: helpers and transition forces lead the boat, trust is the main safety technique here, accompanied by the rites of change of place: housewarming, clearing space, wires of the dead stage of life. If life calls somewhere quieter, it is not a regression, but a route.

The boat is circling in place or backing up: the crossing hung over due to unfinished cargo, fear of a new shore or a secret desire to return.

☉ Golden Dawn tradition

«The Lord of Earned Success» — Lord of Deserved Success

Mercury gets a systematic and detached intelligence in Aquarius, and the six harmonizes suit: the output is not through emotion, but through intelligent method, calculation and route.

✧ Archetype and core meaning

The boat is worth leaving, leaving, leaving, going to leave, going to leave for a long time and seriously, but every Monday everything is postponed, or backtracking: circumstances pull back — the offer to return the position, the call of the ex, pity for the one left.

A common reason for towing is unclosed cargo: unsettled goodbyes, unpaid debts, uncollected documents. Until the tail of the past is legally and emotionally decorated, the new shore holds its distance. And sometimes vice versa, flight to escape: cities change because it seems that the problem is geography. What you're running from is sitting in the same boat.

🏠 Daily life

The suitcase is half-packed, the sale announcement hangs for weeks, repairs are started and abandoned. Small incompleteness multiplies — unwritten answers, uncapped calls. You may be forced to return from a trip or live together again "while temporarily." Bring to the end one thing that pulls water, the move will return.

💼 Work and career

The transition is frozen: the offer is received, it is scary to sign; the dismissal is announced, the deadlines move endlessly. The project is transferred back and forth, exhausting the team. There are possible difficulties with the documents for moving. Write down everything that should be completed before leaving, and close the list — otherwise the old place will hold by the sleeve.

❤ Relationships

It's a breakup that never ends: things are cruising, the last conversation is in its fourth month, they both don't know if they're together or not, or the couple are hanging between the stations, putting off the next stage, and it takes honesty: either swim or admit that you want to row back, the middle of the river is not housing.

🌿 Health

The recovery stalls: the course is interrupted in the middle — "it became easier, quit the pills," and the disease comes back in waves. Rehabilitation does not go because the nervous environment is the same. The diagnosis is re-calibrated, the prescriptions change. The recipe: complete the treatment and remove the source, not just the symptoms.

🔮 Spiritual meaning

The practice of crossing is blocked: the rites on the road crumble, because the petitioner has not decided whether to leave; the spirits of the place are held for debts; the ritual of completion is necessary - an official goodbye to the place, people, yourself.

🌿 How the school reads this rank

The main idea

Help by action. It's not enough to advise, you have to do for someone else what they don't have the strength to do right now.

How it manifests

In work, taking on a part of the task and making life really easy for the other person, in relationships, supporting with a specific action, in development, passing on the experience after you win.

The shadow side

Help becomes intervention and becomes a disservice.

Keywords

assistance · action · support · intervention

📜 In detail — from the rank lecture

✧ Essence of the card

We do for a person what he can not do: find a visa to Poland instead of Googled, transport a woman with a child in a boat, “who physically does not have the strength to row.”

☀ Upright position
  • Social/work: it is faster for a beginner to find a document than to explain; balance is to understand where real help is needed, and where it is enough to prompt information.
  • Finance: the distinction of suits – “money is a pentacle (resource), and the way to influence the situation is swords”; a man can pay for two or “chop wood to the owner of the tavern” – the impact of actions.
  • Relationship: straight – a man pays for a girl in a restaurant “to show strength and wealth”, helps carry a heavy door; upside down – “help me, I’m weak” with a door that she will open, and the argument “a man should pay”, although “when we are colleagues, everyone pays for themselves”.
  • Self-development: the victory of the five is not final, knowledge to update - "run as hard as possible to stay in place"; learned to win - help another.
  • On Waite’s interpretation (“going on a road with mixed feelings”): “the main meaning of the card is to help another person by your actions”; swords in a boat are only “protection from danger.”
☾ Negative meaning

• force intervention – a “bear service” by an uninvited rescuer (“a tick in the diary of good deeds”), the decision is for another; or you are manipulated – the partner “does not understand computers”, and sits deftly in social networks.

★ School emphases and distinctive points
  • “Wait did not draw much for nothing”: the scales of the merchant refer to Justice and “a balanced decision”.
  • The animals in the cards are “our animal aspirations”; the color of the horse encodes the motif (white is the light side, black is the selfish side).
  • Color code: red - matter ("red rose - emblem of the material world", wine - blood of Christ), white - spirit ("water from the wound of Christ" with a spear of fate); white flowers - unselfishness.
  • Slavic series: sayings, compassionate grandmothers, “plombir 20 kopecks”; Soviet cartoons – an uncle from the factory of gutalin (“sends someone without getting hit”) and a cartoon about a bouquet (“for what?” – “just so!”).
  • Modern images: Google (“he can’t Google”), Facebook, slipping friends, comments “School of Modern Tarot, you are the best tarologist” as an illustration of an inverted six wands.
  • Techniques: argue with the classic correctly ("I do not use the author of the book, give my vision"); anti-detailing - "simplify... details can distort the general meaning"; "history is an example, not an instruction"; chess pattern and the baton of the triumphant without leaves - only hypotheses.
🗝 Keywords

Do for a man, help by action, decision for another, disservice.
