Tarot / Justice

Major Arcana · XI

XIJustice

Element: Air  ·  Astrology: Libra

fairnesstruthresponsibilitycause and effectlawimpartialitybalance

The arc of honesty: nothing disappears without a trace, and the decision will be made on the merits, not on sympathy.

✧ Archetype and core meaning

Justice is the lark of precision calculation. The sword in the raised hand says, the decision is final, the scales in the left hand say that the cold logic is balanced by intuition. In the world of this card, nothing dissolves in the air: every deed, word, even unspoken intention returns to its author.

When it falls, the time to pretend is over, and the circumstances are such that the facts fall into place, and with them you have to acknowledge your own role in what happened, not punishment or tribunal, but the restoration of broken order.

The arcana associated with Libra teaches you to see the dispute on both sides and choose not convenient, but the right one.

🏠 Daily life

The day is good for settling the agreements: pay back the debt, close the formalities, re-read the terms of the contract before signing. The overdue domestic dispute — about money, schedule, duties — will be resolved by discussing the case, not feelings. Everything that is now divided equally, will no longer be a cause for grievances.

💼 Work and career

Cases require legal precision: write down agreements, prepare for audits, certifications, or compensation negotiations, and the outcome will be objective, especially if your hands are clean. The card protects lawyers, auditors, intermediaries, and anyone who makes personnel decisions: the promotion will come as a measure of merit.

❤ Relationships

Relationships are tested for honesty, and it's time to talk about not who loves whom more, but whether the union is fair: who invests, who uses, what everyone really expects, and it takes away the accumulated grievances better than any reconciliation, and promises lonely people to meet with a mature and decent person — often associated with law or older.

🌿 Health

The body reminds us of balance: exercise and rest must balance each other, otherwise the body will count. Attention zone — kidneys, lower back, skin; a good moment for examination and a second, sober opinion instead of self-diagnosis. Recovery is now smooth, if you follow the doctor's prescriptions.

🔮 Spiritual meaning

Esoterically, it's the arcanum of karmic accounting: the evening inventory of the day -- without excuses or self-flagellation -- is markedly leveling events. The scales of the figure are correlated with the Egyptian Maat, whose pen weighs the soul after death. In practice, the image of the sword is used to cut off lies: the point cuts illusions, and the firm hand holds only what you really own.

Libra tilted: bias, avoidance of uncomfortable issues and decisions made in other people's interests.

✧ Archetype and core meaning

Inverted Justice is a trial that has been held without you or against you, and someone else's merits are written in someone else's column, someone else's mistakes are hanging on you.

Often, the card describes the internal accuser: a person judges himself more severely than any tribunal, drags not his cargo - or, conversely, self-justifies and shifts the blame outside until the lesson is learned.

Arcan warns that an attempt to circumvent the law – state, human or karmic – will result in a double count.

🏠 Daily life

There may be confusion in documents and promises: double-check bills, receipts, oral agreements — anything that is not fixed risks being interpreted against you. Disputes with utilities and sellers will take longer than it would cost. Large purchases postpone: today's price does not reflect values.

💼 Work and career

There's a lot of unfairness at work, with the bonus being sympathetic, the mistake being overblown, the idea being misnamed, the formal process getting dragged out or complicated -- keep copies of documents and get a lawyer in. A separate warning is for you: a little bit of "trick" with reporting will end up being checked, and one-sided decisions today are better not made at all.

❤ Relationships

The pair accumulates a score of mutual claims: one gives, the other takes for granted, and both keep silent about inequality. The past is used as a weapon in current disputes, jealousy is dressed up as care - such an exchange "justly" kills intimacy. It can be a heavy division of property and mutual friends.

🌿 Health

The symptoms confuse you and the doctors: diagnoses change one by one, treatment fails to work — look for the reason deeper than the first appointments. The body signals chronic imbalance: unilateral exertion, lack of sleep, attempts to drown out stress with alcohol. Second opinion is mandatory, self-medication now plays the role of a biased judge.

🔮 Spiritual meaning

In magical work, the card points to unclosed karmic accounts, tribal stories where someone has been lied to or stripped of their own, and cleansing won't work until they've done an honest review of their own actions: first confession, then absolution. The amulets of justice are now weak; the most powerful thing is not to participate in other people's intrigues.

🌿 In brief

The main idea

Justice is more accurately understood as justice. It's a card of a contract between two equal parties, each with its own interests, rights and obligations.

How it manifests

Treaties, promises, commitments, partnerships, legal issues and the need to honor agreements reached.

Work and money

Documents, contracts, courts, lawyers, official registration.

In a relationship

Marriage: A legally binding contract between two people.

The shadow side

Justice doesn't become evil; it just starts working against the person; the debt has to be paid back, whether it's convenient or not.

Feature of the school

The card is linked to Libra and the VII House, and the main formula is "Aries is me, Libra is you."

📜 In detail — lecture extracts

✧ Essence of the card

He reads the card through English Justice → Latin Justice, the goddess of treaties: the core of the card is “the feeling of a contract between two equal parties”, judicial and contractual matters. In Russian, he would call the card “Justice”: justice and justice for the inhabitants of the former CIS “either with slight bewilderment or with loud laughter” cannot coincide – the card about the divine principle of justice, which is distorted by imperfect people (“the divine essence is distorted in our material world”). In his system of correspondence, the VII house, the sign of Libra, two planets, “the feeling of harmony in Hydronesodocularity” is needed to put together in the “the root of life.”

☀ Upright position

There's no Slavic row.

The common series: partnership, competition with the opposite party, obligations, a promise made, a contract — even if it is verbal: “as agreed and should be fulfilled, you call yourself a gardem, get in the back.” Libra = someone else’s point of view: your interests on one cup, the interests of another person on the other. Waite quotes: the card “strictly follows traditional symbolism” and like the High Priestess sits between the columns, but this “moral principle that rewards everyone according to his merits” is strict justice of agreements, not the priestess’ spiritual court.

Work/finance: paperwork – written by people perceive as a fixed truth; when lending money or giving away property, fix the obligations documented ("Iron Paper of Armor" Preobrazhensky) – otherwise "then you will prove what to what." The court card points to it almost literally. Profession: lawyer or client; renting an apartment is an official contract. Joint property, communal housing: squeeze in freedoms, occupying only your half of the plate (personal housing lives in the opposite house of Aries).

Relationships: emotional peace of mind, as it worked, is not the highest justice: you treat a person, so does he, you. You accept other people's wishes. The card is only a formal marriage: a seal, a registry office or a town hall, witnesses, a documented promise; traditional marriage is the Hierophant, prolongation of the family "like great-grandfathers." In California, Justice can fall on an unconventional marriage — it is legally binding: you can negotiate anything.

Self-development: service and help (see partner needs: "because we're brothers, my problems are your problems"), the second half of life is giving people what I've accumulated. There's also the personal benefit of being a good pioneer - sincere duplicity; "a bad peace is better than a good war," any smile is better than no one.

☾ Negative meaning

The formula: justice in the negative is "the same as ordinary justice ... it is in any case present," just works against you. borrowed $10,000 for a year and gave it away - straight; the year has passed, you should give, and there is no money - justice is already against you, "but it is still present" conveniently or not. The disadvantages of qualities are concretized along the way: bureaucracy is the clearly named negative aspect of the card (twruffle what you need to do quickly, paint the nuances of the contract; learn the details, otherwise Nemesis will punish); the skortov - justice is already against you, "but it is conveniently present" for you, you or you are not interested in the question: "you are not thinking about it" - you, you can forget about yourself in the carvets, you, you, you can forget about it, you can easily forget about it, you can forget about yourself.
