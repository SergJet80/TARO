⚷ Chiron astrological factor

The wounded healer, healing

♈ Zodiac Signs ☉ Planets 🎡 Wheel

Energy

Vulnerability, healing, mentorship, joining opposites.

Character of the energy

A particular sensitivity to one’s own sense of ‘inadequacy’ and an ability to understand another person’s pain.

Influence on life

Chiron points to an enduring inner wound that may not disappear entirely but can become a source of wisdom, a gift for helping, and self-acceptance.

In its constructive expression

Healing ability, humor, compassion, the ability to accompany others.

In its shadow

Fixation on pain, the role of rescuer or victim, avoidance of closeness.

Formula

‘I heal through acceptance.’
