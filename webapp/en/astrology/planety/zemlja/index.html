⊕ Earth astrological factor

Embodiment, body, support

♈ Zodiac Signs ☉ Planets 🎡 Wheel

Energy

Matter, the body, nature, stability, practical embodiment.

Character of the energy

Realism and a need for security and connection with the body and the surrounding world.

Influence on life

In geocentric astrology, Earth is not usually interpreted as a separate planet in the natal chart. Within Western esotericism, it symbolizes the ability to ground ideas, care for the body, and turn an intention into a result.

In its constructive expression

Reliability, patience, fruitfulness, environmental awareness.

In its shadow

Inertia, materialism, fear of change, excessive control.

Formula

‘I embody.’
