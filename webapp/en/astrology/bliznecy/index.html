♊ Gemini zodiac sign

Contact, movement, information

♈ Zodiac Signs ☉ Planets 🎡 Wheel

In brief

‘I need to understand what is happening around me.’

What this person is like

Gemini learns about the world through contact and information. Conversation, reading, news, travel, new acquaintances, and exchanging opinions help them feel alive. They learn quickly, switch easily, and often have several interests at once.

What drives them

Interest. When they are interested, they grasp things instantly. When they are bored, holding their attention is difficult.

Strengths

Curiosity, flexibility, sociability, quick adaptation, an ability to connect people and information, and the ability to explain difficult things in simple words.

Weaknesses

Scattered attention, fussiness, lack of focus, and a habit of starting several things at once.

In relationships

Conversation matters greatly. They need a partner with whom they can exchange thoughts, laugh, and keep discovering something new.

At work and with money

They suit fields involving information, education, trade, sales, mediation, languages, media, the internet, and negotiation.

When things go wrong

They become restless, reach for fresh impressions, and use them to avoid dealing with a serious problem.

Main inner conflict

They want to know everything, but real depth requires setting aside at least some of their other interests for a while.

This is a general description of a Sun sign, not a complete portrait of a person. In an actual natal chart, character depends on the Sun and other factors. Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a scientifically established prediction; it is not a diagnosis or financial advice.
