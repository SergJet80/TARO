♈ Zodiac Signs ☉ Planets 🎡 Wheel

The zodiac wheel is a circle of 12 signs to which this system connects the Tarot Major Arcana through astrological houses and planets. The Fool (0) and the World (XXI) stand outside the wheel and frame the journey. The Fool is the entry point, where ‘nothing has begun’; the World completes the cycle and marks the exit. Between them, 20 Major Arcana follow the circle. Select a sign to see its Arcana and description. Turn the wheel with the buttons or by selecting a sign.
