♉ Taurus zodiac sign

Stability, support, form

♈ Zodiac Signs ☉ Planets 🎡 Wheel

In brief

‘It has to become real.’

What this person is like

Taurus experiences the world through practicality and stability. It is important to them not merely to imagine something but to create something reliable, comfortable, and durable. They are unhurried and patient, have a good sense of the material value of things, and usually dislike abrupt change without a clear reason.

What drives them

A need for stability, security, and a firm foundation. Taurus gradually creates an understandable world around themselves: habits, possessions, people, a profession, and a way of life.

Strengths

Patience, reliability, practicality, endurance, the ability to work toward a result for a long time, and the ability to preserve and strengthen what has been achieved.

Weaknesses

Inertia and stubbornness. They may hold on to a relationship, job, or habit for too long simply because it is familiar.

In relationships

They value reliability, physical closeness, comfort, and loyalty. They often express love through concrete actions: care, help, and comfort. The other side is possessiveness and jealousy.

At work and with money

They do well in material and practical tasks, know how to save and plan, and can build a stable enterprise. They like to see a concrete result.

When things go wrong

They shut down, dig in their heels, and resist any change.

Main inner conflict

They seek to create security but sometimes turn that security into a cage.

This is a general description of a Sun sign, not a complete portrait of a person. In an actual natal chart, character depends on the Sun and other factors. Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a scientifically established prediction; it is not a diagnosis or financial advice.
