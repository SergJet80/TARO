♏ Scorpio zodiac sign

Depth, power, transformation

♈ Zodiac Signs ☉ Planets 🎡 Wheel

In brief

‘What is hidden beneath the surface?’

What this person is like

Scorpio has an intense inner life. They are interested in the hidden causes of events, people’s real motives, crises, power, attachment, and transformation. A superficial answer rarely satisfies them.

What drives them

A need to reach the essence.

Strengths

Psychological insight, resilience, the ability to withstand crises, depth of feeling, and an ability to rebuild themselves completely after a difficult experience.

Weaknesses

Suspicion, jealousy, possessiveness, and a drive to control and verify.

In relationships

Half-measures rarely satisfy them. Trust and closeness can run very deep, but broken trust is difficult to restore.

At work and with money

They do well where work involves complex, hidden, crisis-related, or investigative processes.

When things go wrong

They try to control the situation at any price; jealousy, manipulation, and checking appear.

Main inner conflict

They seek absolute closeness, yet trusting another person may be the hardest thing of all.

This is a general description of a Sun sign, not a complete portrait of a person. In an actual natal chart, character depends on the Sun and other factors. Astrology is a symbolic tradition, not a scientifically established prediction; it is not a diagnosis or financial advice.
